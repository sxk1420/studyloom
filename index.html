 THE RULES, IN PLAIN SIGHT

A suggestion you can question.

This is a rule-based prototype, not a trained model. It cannot predict your grades, attention or learning outcomes.

How tasks are ranked

Urgency: overdue/today = 6, tomorrow = 5, 2–3 days = 3, later = 1. Support: low confidence = 3, medium = 2, high = 1. Balanced score = urgency + support, allowing low confidence to outweigh a small urgency difference. Deadline mode uses 3 × urgency + support, giving urgency greater weight.

“Do first” overrides scores until you release it. Ties use the earlier deadline, then creation order. Each included task receives at most one block, capped at your selected maximum. Five-minute breaks separate blocks. A break is added only if at least five minutes of study can follow. Unused time may remain.

How adaptation works

After at least five active timer minutes, three page departures or two hidden minutes trigger a tentative suggestion for 15-minute blocks. Timing includes hidden time, excludes manual pauses, and is not a measure of focused study. The thresholds and weights are unvalidated design assumptions.

Suggestions appear when you finish a block, not while you study. You can reject the interpretation, accept a shorter maximum, change it manually, or undo acceptance. Nothing automatically marks a task complete or reduces its estimate. After finishing a live block, Review progress lets you record completion or a new remaining-work estimate, preview the next plan and explicitly apply it. The next plan uses your current available-time setting; elapsed time is not automatically deducted.

What is remembered

Only the current tab holds your tasks, latest block observations, decision and one previous plan for Undo. An optional saved copy of tasks and planning settings uses local storage on this browser. Save is explicit, later edits are not automatically saved, and Restore is explicit. There is no server database. Observations and decisions are never persisted. Forget observations also clears the decision and undo history so forgotten observations cannot return. Reload clears the current session; a saved plan remains available to restore until you delete it or clear browser storage. Exports are your own separate files.

Demonstration mode

The interrupted-session example injects a fixed observation: 10 elapsed minutes, 3 departures, 2 minutes hidden. It is explicitly simulated, not a live measurement. The same inference function handles live and simulated observations.
